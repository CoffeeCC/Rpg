Lighting

The room lamp is not in the fiction — it is the lamp above the table the board is sitting on. Raking from the near-left is what keeps it out: the board's own border blocks shadow the dungeon from it entirely, so it lands on the table, the rim and the frame and stops at the wall. Drag it to 0 to see why the slab edge needs it.

Flame size is the penumbra: at 0 the shadows are knife-edged, because a light with no width cannot cast a soft edge.

Pieces

Drag Piece lift and watch the shadow WIDEN AND FADE together. Widening alone reads as the piece growing; the fade is what reads as a lift.

Inlay

The chamfer is what makes a seam an EDGE rather than a drawn line — but push it past ~0.5 and it tips a floor tile so far from the lantern that the seam goes black, and the grid reads as rings in the pool.
